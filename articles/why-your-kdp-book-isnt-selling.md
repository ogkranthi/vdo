# Why Your KDP Book Isn't Selling

My books failed. Not because of bad content. Not because of ugly covers. They
just sat there, invisible, while other books in the same niche sold every day.

It took me three published books to understand the difference, and it had
nothing to do with quality. It came down to one decision made before writing a
single word.

I wrote what I wanted to write. Smart publishers write what the market is
already searching for. That's the entire gap.

Your book doesn't need to be better. It needs to be findable. That means a
specific, searchable keyword baked into the title and subtitle — the exact
phrase a real buyer types into Amazon's search bar.

Before you write, validate the keyword with real market data. Type it into
Amazon. Look at the results. Are books in this niche actually selling (check
their best-seller rank)? Is the competition beatable (fewer than a thousand
results, weak covers, thin reviews)?

The sweet spot is a niche with proven sales AND low competition. That
combination is rarer than you'd think, and finding it is the real work of
publishing — the writing comes second.

Make that one shift — keyword first, book second — and your next book gets
found instead of buried.
